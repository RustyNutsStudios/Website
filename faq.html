Questions

What is EGGREGIOUS?

A top-down Soviet sci-fi survivors roguelite for one player. You recruit an elite team of specialists and hold the line against a horde of thousands. Unlock weapons and items, level everyone up, and build a strategy that is yours.

It is made in Unreal Engine 5 by Rusty Nuts Studios. The EGGREGIOUS page has the art and the first minute.

What is the playtest?

A build of the game in players' hands before release. You play, we watch what breaks and what works, and the game changes because of it.

Keys come through Discord. It is not the finished game, and it is not the demo.

How do I join the playtest?

Invites open this week on Discord. The PLAY THE TEST button on the EGGREGIOUS page goes live the moment the invite does.

Then: join the Discord, find the playtest channel, grab a key, play it, and tell us what broke.
