Post-Soviet survivors-like where you recruit an elite team of shock troops and bring liberation to the solar system. You are the first soldier to land. But you will not be the last. Call in your comrades and hold the line against thousands of bugs until the main force lands.

Wishlist on Steam

  • CHOOSE WHO LANDS FIRST. 30+ elite units, from the Field Cook to the Tank Driver.

  • EQUIP YOURSELF AND LEVEL UP. 90+ weapons (Chainsaw, Flamethrower, Frying Pan), up to 6 firing at once. Level up until the bugs are the ones calling for help.

  • REQUEST YOUR COMRADES. They drop from the sky, fully armed, and fight without being told.

  • LEAD YOUR TEAM. Where you go, your squad goes. Capture points of interest: radio beacons and the golden chest.

  • FIND POWER-UPS. Chests, weapon cases and 140+ items: a Telescope for more range, a Padded Vest for more health, Jet Army Boots for more speed.

  • CREATE SYNERGIES. The Ammo Bearer gives every gun near him one more shot. The Cutthroat makes every kill heal; the Scout reveals the map.

  • DEFEND AGAINST THE HORDE, DAY AND NIGHT. When night falls the horde grows and a boss comes with it. 20-minute runs.

Victory is mandatory.
